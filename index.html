Open without browser bars

Fullscreen is not available in this browser view. On iPhone, you can open this tour as a Home Screen web app:

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Share.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.
  4. Open the new Apartment icon on your Home Screen.

The web app opens without Safari’s address bar. System indicators may remain. Use the Home gesture to leave the app.
